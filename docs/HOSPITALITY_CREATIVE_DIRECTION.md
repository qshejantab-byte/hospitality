# Hospitality Growth: creative direction (v3): "The View"

30 October 2026. This replaces both Sunpath (v1) and Arrivals (v2). Arrivals is abandoned completely: no split-flap boards, no brass hardware, no hotel-prop metaphors.

## The idea

Every hospitality business sells a view: a room, a terrace, a table, a morning. Guests decide to come because of what they can *see*, long before they arrive. TODO Growth makes sure the right people see it, understand it, ask about it and book it.

The page is designed like a contemporary hospitality editorial: calm stone backgrounds, high-contrast serif typography, generous space, and one recurring visual form, **the arch**. An arch is the frame through which a guest first sees a place: a window, a doorway, a loggia. On this page each arch frames something meaningful:

- the view (hero);
- each service (What we do);
- client stories (Work);
- the closing invitation (footer).

There is no fake photography. The arches contain drawn, layered landscapes and fine line illustrations, labelled where they illustrate.

## Visual system

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | **Bodoni Moda** (display: high-contrast editorial serif, italic for emphasis) and **Manrope** (interface and body) |
| Palette | Limestone `#F3EEE6`, sand `#E7DFD2`, deep forest ink `#1C2320` (text and dark chapters), forest `#22362D`, terracotta `#B5562F` (one accent, used sparingly) |
| Form | The arch, plus fine 1px rules, chapter numbers (01–07) and wide margins. No cards, no glows, no glass. |
| Images | Layered SVG landscapes (hills, lake, light) inside arches; single-weight line drawings for services |
| Motion | Slow and deliberate: headline lines rise from a mask; the view parallaxes gently with the pointer; line drawings draw themselves; the guest-flow animation runs only while visible. Everything else stays still. Reduced motion shows final frames. |

## Five redesigned areas

1. **Navigation.** Transparent on the stone hero, then a slim limestone bar with a hairline and a reading-progress line. The links are numbered chapters, and the active chapter is underlined. On mobile a full-screen limestone menu slides down, with large serif chapter links.
2. **Hero.** An asymmetric editorial composition:
   - The headline is set in four deliberate lines.
   - A tall arch holds the view: Rwanda-inspired hills, a lake, morning light.
   - Four fine annotations link to the arch (Discover · Explore · Enquire · Book), each naming the service behind it. The page's offer is legible at a glance.
3. **Missed arrivals: "The leaky journey."** A quiet data-art piece:
   - Small dots (guests) flow from left to right through five gates (Discovery, Exploration, Enquiry, Booking, Pre-arrival).
   - At each gate some drift away and fade. Few reach the arch, "your room".
   - One switch, *Leaky / Connected*, shows the same interest when the gaps are closed: the arch fills with light.
   - It's labelled as an illustration, with no numbers.
4. **What we do: an editorial index.** Six services grouped under Attract, Convert and Serve:
   - Each row opens to a short explanation and the business value.
   - A sticky arch on the right draws a line illustration of the open service.
5. **Footer: the final chapter.** A full-width closing statement framed by an arch outline that draws itself, then the CTA. Below that, a clean, typographic footer: positioning, chapter navigation, contact (shown only once confirmed in config), and back to top.

## Remaining sections, re-set in the same system

The guest journey, work, directory, how-we-work and enquiry sections keep their content and functionality, re-typeset in the same limestone, serif and arch language.

## Honesty

- Illustrations are labelled.
- No client photography, testimonials or figures.
- Grotta and Eagleview appear only in local review mode.
- The contact details stay flagged as unconfirmed.

## v4 elevation: photography (30 Oct 2026)

The View keeps its strategy, palette and type; the arch is now used selectively: as the **aperture** through which the hero and closing photographs open, the services drawing frame and the reserved client-story frame. Everything else is photographic or typographic.

- **Hero**: full-bleed cinematic photograph (Muhavura volcano through tree silhouettes), opened from an arch aperture while the camera settles; headline rises line by line; value line + CTA; journey strip along the base naming the service behind each step.
- **Interlude** (new): full-bleed Lake Kivu photograph between Missed arrivals and the journey.
- **Who it’s for**: one large photographic stage with five kinds of business; selecting one wipes in its photograph and copy. Mobile: each category is its own photo-then-text scene.
- **Closing + footer**: full-bleed Virunga photograph with the closing line, then a dark, organised footer with photo credits.
- **Motion family**: open (aperture), settle (scale), rise (type), drift (scroll), wipe (category change). Reduced motion shows final states.
- **Assets**: Creative Commons stock photographs from Wikimedia Commons, credited on the page and in `docs/ASSET_CREDITS.md`. None depicts a TODO client. No video (no verifiable licensed source reachable).
